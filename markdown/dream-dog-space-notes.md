Ever sit down and write out a dream like you’re trying to make it real just by putting it on paper?

I’ve been doing that lately. I’ve always wanted to build a space where **people and dogs** can hang out together—not a quick stop, but a real third place. Picture dog boarding that doesn’t feel like a warehouse, a small park where pups and their humans can actually chill, and a tiny café where both can sit, relax, or get some work done without anyone getting side-eyed.

Right now it’s all notes. Pages of them. Exciting when I read them back, and honestly a little scary, because I have **no idea where to start**: permits, money, location, whether the café idea is naive or the whole point.

If you’ve ever built something from nothing—or even tried and stalled—I’d love to hear what helped you take the first step. No advice is too small.
